THE IDEA

A cookbook that behaves like an editorial scroll.

Instead of separating recipes into app screens, Berry Vibes Studio stays on one continuous page. Each recipe becomes a visual chapter: a split composition, a centered recipe ticket, a flavor note, and a full recipe panel when you want the details.
